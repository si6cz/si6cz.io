Word Explorers: privacy policy

Word Explorers is an Alexa skill that helps kids learn Spanish and French words.

We collect nothing. The skill does not ask for, record or store any personal information, voice recordings, names, ages or locations. It has no accounts, no ads, no links and no purchases.

During a game, the current question and score are kept only for that session so the game can continue. They are deleted when the session ends.

Amazon processes voice requests as described in Amazon's own Alexa privacy notice. Parents can review and delete voice recordings in the Alexa app.

Questions: contact the developer through the skill's page in the Alexa Skills Store.
